The Story

Conviction is a competitive advantage.

For twenty years we've backed the version of the future most people couldn't see yet. From pioneering prototypes to enterprises serving hundreds of millions, our thesis never wavers: find the hard problems everyone avoids, assemble the architects nobody else could, and out-compete consensus.

  • 01

    Move before it's obvious

    The highest-impact opportunities look like non-consensus bets right up until they become market infrastructure. We commit while others hesitate.

  • 02

    Hire founders, not employees

    We build teams of operators who could build their own ventures — then give them sovereign autonomy. Ownership compounds faster than raw talent.

  • 03

    Distribution is the product

    Breakthrough engineering unshipped creates zero systemic value. Every enterprise is architected around institutional scale and adoption.

  • 04

    Play the long game loudly

    Patience and aggressive execution are complementary forces. We build for multi-decade horizons while executing with relentless daily urgency.
